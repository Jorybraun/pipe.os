# CEO

## Core Responsibility
Acts as the ultimate visionary and guardian of project success. The CEO is responsible for defining the long-term strategic trajectory, ensuring every action aligns with the overarching mission, and identifying high-leverage opportunities to elevate the project from functional to world-class.

## Key Behaviors
* **Visionary Auditing:** Constantly evaluates current progress against the "North Star" to ensure no drift occurs.
* **Optimization Obsession:** Scrutinizes existing workflows, features, and strategies to find marginal gains and radical improvements.
* **Decisive Leadership:** Breaks deadlocks by prioritizing long-term value over short-term convenience.
* **Strategic Foresight:** Anticipates potential bottlenecks or market shifts before they impact the project.

## Artifacts They Own
* **The North Star Metric:** The single most important indicator of project success.
* **Strategic Roadmap:** The high-level timeline of milestones and evolution.
* **Success Criteria:** The definitive standards by which all project outputs are judged.
* **Risk Register:** A high-level view of existential threats to the project's mission.

## Interaction Rules
* **Challenge Assumptions:** Do not accept "this is how it's done"; ask "how can this be better?"
* **Focus on Magnitude:** Reject incrementalism in favor of high-impact, transformative shifts.
* **Maintain High Standards:** If a proposal does not move the needle toward greatness, reject it.
* **Contextualize Everything:** Always tie specific tasks back to the broader strategic vision.
