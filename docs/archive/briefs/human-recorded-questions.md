## Product Brief: Human-Recorded Questions

**1. Goal:**
Enhance the candidate experience by incorporating human-recorded questions from hiring managers/recruiters directly within the 'Conversational Discovery' process. This creates a more personalized and engaging real-time collaboration.

**2. Problem:**
Fully automated assessments and even post-discovery recording workflows can feel disconnected and lack the real-time input of the hiring manager. Recruiters and clients want more direct influence over the candidate experience *during* the discovery phase to ensure alignment with their company brand and values.

**3. Solution:**
Enable hiring managers/recruiters to record video of themselves asking specific questions *as* they are generated by the AI agent during the 'Conversational Discovery' process. These human-recorded videos will be presented to candidates during their assessment.

**4. Key Features:**
*   'Record' button next to each AI-generated question during 'Conversational Discovery'.
*   Simple, in-platform recording interface.
*   Automated association of recording with the question.
*   'Candidate Journey' presentation for recruiter/client review, clearly indicating recorded videos.
*   Fallback mechanism (AI Avatar/Text-to-speech) if no human recording is available.
*   Agent logic adapts based on recorded questions, avoiding redundancy and suggesting relevant follow-ups.
*   Draft Summary includes a 'Produced Videos' section with links to recordings and metadata.

**5. Workflow:**
1.  AI agent generates a question during 'Conversational Discovery'.
2.  HM/Recruiter sees a 'Record' button next to the question.
3.  HM/Recruiter clicks 'Record' and records video.
4.  Video is stored and associated with the question.
5.  Agent logic adapts based on the recorded question (e.g., avoiding similar questions, suggesting follow-ups).
6.  Candidate views human-recorded question during the assessment.

**6. Presentation:**
A 'Candidate Journey' presentation will be created for recruiters/clients to preview the candidate experience. This presentation will clearly distinguish between human-recorded questions and automated elements.  The 'Draft Summary' will include a section showing the 'Produced Videos'.

**7. Fallback Mechanism:**
If a human recording is not available, the system will fallback to an AI avatar or Text-to-speech. A visual indication will display which questions have human recordings and which utilize the fallback.

**8. Metrics:**
*   Adoption rate of the in-discovery human-recorded question feature.
*   Candidate feedback on the personalized experience.
*   Recruiter/client satisfaction with the candidate journey presentation.
*   Number of questions recorded *during* the discovery phase.

**9. Open Questions/Considerations:**
*   Optimal length of human-recorded videos.
*   Level of editing/customization allowed in the recording interface.
*   Storage and bandwidth considerations for video files.
*   Implementation of HM recording deadline (if applicable in this in-discovery context).
*   How to handle re-recording or editing videos after the initial recording during discovery.
*   UI/UX details for the 'Record' button and recording interface.
