## Dimension weights

- ownership: 1.0
- collaboration: 1.2
- learning-orientation: 1.0
- conflict-handling: 1.4
- self-awareness: 1.3

## Preferred question tags

- direct-reports
- performance-conversations
- hiring-decisions
- difficult-feedback
- team-health
- trade-off-decisions

## Deprioritized tags

- solo-deep-work
- individual-technical-debugging

## Rationale

Managers are evaluated on their ability to navigate hard conversations (conflict handling weighted highest) and on their awareness of how they land on direct reports (self-awareness weighted second). Collaboration is elevated because managers are measured by the output of their team, not themselves.
