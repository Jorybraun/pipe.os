# Learning Orientation

**Type:** competency scoring dimension
**Scale:** 1-5 BARS
**Evidence required:** verbatim quotes from transcript

---

## What we're measuring

How the candidate responds to not knowing something. Strong learning orientation looks like: admitting ignorance quickly, naming specific gaps, describing concrete learning actions with outcomes, and showing that recent work has changed their mental model of something.

Weak learning orientation looks like: performing expertise, describing learning as a passive byproduct of doing the job, inability to name anything they were wrong about recently, and framing "I don't know" as a threat.

---

## What this is NOT

- Not "having lots of certifications" — certifications measure completion, not orientation.
- Not "reading a lot" — consumption without change is not learning.
- Not "changing jobs often" — novelty is not learning.

---

## BARS anchors

- **5** — Candidate describes a specific recent instance where they were wrong about something, names what changed in their understanding, and connects it to a concrete action. Uses phrases like "I used to think X, now I think Y because Z."
- **4** — Describes a deliberate learning effort with a clear outcome. Can name what they knew before vs. after.
- **3** — Describes learning on the job in general terms. Some specifics. No clear before/after shift.
- **2** — Describes learning in vague terms or as inevitable byproduct of experience. Cannot name a specific recent shift in understanding.
- **1** — Performs expertise. Cannot name anything they were wrong about. Treats "I don't know" as a threat to be deflected.
