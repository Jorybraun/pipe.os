# Self-Awareness

**Type:** competency scoring dimension
**Scale:** 1-5 BARS
**Evidence required:** verbatim quotes from transcript

---

## What we're measuring

The candidate's ability to see themselves accurately — strengths, weaknesses, and blind spots. Strong self-awareness looks like: naming real weaknesses without softening, describing how their style affects others, recognizing patterns across multiple past situations, and distinguishing "what I did" from "what I wish I had done."

Weak self-awareness looks like: weaknesses that are secretly strengths ("I work too hard"), inability to describe their impact on others, no evidence of pattern recognition across situations, and treating all past decisions as obviously correct.

---

## What this is NOT

- Not "low confidence" — strong self-awareness coexists with strong confidence.
- Not "oversharing" — performative vulnerability is not awareness.
- Not "self-deprecation" — putting yourself down can be its own form of avoidance.

---

## BARS anchors

- **5** — Candidate names a real weakness with a concrete example of it costing them something. Describes how their style lands on specific other people. Names a pattern they've seen across multiple past situations.
- **4** — Names a real weakness with an example. Some evidence of impact-awareness.
- **3** — Names a weakness but it is mild, or presented in a way that softens it. Limited evidence of impact-awareness.
- **2** — Names a "weakness" that is actually a strength in disguise ("I'm a perfectionist"). No genuine vulnerability.
- **1** — Cannot name a weakness. Or: attributes all past difficulties to others. Or: every past decision is framed as obviously correct.
