# Ownership

**Type:** competency scoring dimension
**Scale:** 1-5 BARS
**Evidence required:** verbatim quotes from transcript

---

## What we're measuring

The degree to which the candidate takes personal responsibility for outcomes — including outcomes they did not cause, outcomes that are ambiguous, and outcomes outside their formal scope. Strong ownership looks like: the candidate treats unowned problems as their problem, stays with a problem past the point they could plausibly hand it off, and accepts blame when things go wrong even when blame would be easy to deflect.

Weak ownership looks like: waiting for assignment, stopping at the edge of job description, blaming process or others when things fail, describing past work only in the passive voice.

---

## What this is NOT

- Not "working hard" — hard work without ownership is just grinding.
- Not "having a title" — junior ICs can show extraordinary ownership.
- Not "saying yes to everything" — ownership includes saying no to the wrong work.

---

## BARS anchors (generic — specific rubrics live in each question file)

- **5** — Candidate describes taking responsibility for an outcome outside their formal scope. They name the problem, describe staying with it past the handoff point, and take accountability for the result. Specific quotes about their own decisions and actions.
- **4** — Candidate describes clear ownership within their scope with measurable results. Uses "I" language. Does not deflect when describing failures.
- **3** — Candidate describes their work accurately. Ownership is present but bounded strictly by job description. Some "we" language that doesn't hide their specific contribution.
- **2** — Candidate describes work in mostly passive voice. Unclear what they specifically did vs. what the team did. Deflects responsibility for failures onto process, tools, or others.
- **1** — Candidate cannot produce an example of a problem they owned. Describes work as things that happened to them or were assigned to them. No accountability for outcomes.
