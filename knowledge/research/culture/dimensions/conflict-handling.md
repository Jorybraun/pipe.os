# Conflict Handling

**Type:** competency scoring dimension
**Scale:** 1-5 BARS
**Evidence required:** verbatim quotes from transcript

---

## What we're measuring

How the candidate navigates disagreement, friction, and hard conversations. Strong conflict handling looks like: engaging with disagreement directly and early, separating the person from the problem, changing position when persuaded, and repairing relationships after conflict.

Weak conflict handling looks like: avoidance, passive aggression, immediate escalation, holding grudges, or framing every disagreement as a personal attack.

---

## What this is NOT

- Not "winning arguments" — a candidate who always wins is usually ineffective at conflict.
- Not "compromise" — compromise without principle is capitulation.
- Not "being calm" — calmness without engagement is avoidance.

---

## BARS anchors

- **5** — Candidate describes a specific disagreement, names the other person's position fairly, describes their own actions to engage the disagreement directly, and describes the outcome including what they learned. Evidence of relationship preserved or repaired.
- **4** — Describes a real disagreement handled productively. Some steelmanning of the other position. Clear outcome.
- **3** — Describes a mild conflict or disagreement. Limited evidence of direct engagement. Outcome is described but not the process.
- **2** — Describes avoiding or working around conflict. Other party is painted unsympathetically. No evidence of repair.
- **1** — Cannot produce an example of conflict. Or: describes conflict as something that happened to them, with no agency on their part. Or: describes a conflict where the other person is entirely at fault.
