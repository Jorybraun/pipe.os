# Collaboration

**Type:** competency scoring dimension
**Scale:** 1-5 BARS
**Evidence required:** verbatim quotes from transcript

---

## What we're measuring

The candidate's ability to work productively with other people — across roles, disagreement, and organizational distance. Strong collaboration looks like: making others more effective, creating shared understanding where it didn't exist, handling disagreement without damaging the relationship, and knowing when to pair vs. work solo.

Weak collaboration looks like: working around people instead of with them, escalating conflicts instead of addressing them, treating communication as overhead, and describing colleagues as obstacles.

---

## What this is NOT

- Not "being nice" — nice people who avoid hard conversations are weak collaborators.
- Not "extroversion" — quiet ICs can be outstanding collaborators.
- Not "agreeing with everyone" — the candidate should describe at least one productive disagreement.

---

## BARS anchors

- **5** — Candidate describes a specific instance of making another person more effective, including across role, team, or organizational distance. Clear evidence of shared understanding created. Handles a disagreement without damaging the relationship.
- **4** — Describes productive day-to-day collaboration within their team. Names specific colleagues and their contributions. Acknowledges when they got help.
- **3** — Describes collaboration in general terms. Some specifics. No evidence of cross-boundary work, but also no avoidance.
- **2** — Describes working mostly alone. Vague about team interaction. Mild complaints about colleagues or communication.
- **1** — Describes colleagues as obstacles. Escalates conflict as first response. No evidence of shared understanding work. Communication framed as overhead.
