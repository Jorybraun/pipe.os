# Research Plan: AI Literacy in Hiring

## Core question
Is AI literacy an emerging, assessable skill with a real market opportunity for a hiring platform — and does Pipe's code review + reasoning evaluation surface position it to own that space?

## Sub-questions
1. What is "AI literacy" as a defined concept — what skills/competencies does it include?
2. What does current research say about AI literacy as a workforce skill gap?
3. Are there existing platforms or assessments targeting AI literacy in hiring contexts?
4. What do employers say they want vs. what they're actually testing for?
5. Is "AI literacy" adjacent to what Pipe already tests (code review, reasoning about code) or a genuinely different surface?
6. What is the market size and growth trajectory for AI skills assessment?

## Strategy
- Researcher A: Academic/research angle — AI literacy definitions, competency frameworks, workforce studies
- Researcher B: Competitive landscape — existing platforms, assessments, hiring tools targeting AI skills
- Researcher C: Market signals — employer demand, job postings, VC investment, industry reports

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AI literacy defined with ≥2 independent frameworks or definitions
- [ ] ≥2 sources on workforce skill gap evidence
- [ ] Competitive landscape mapped with ≥3 named platforms/assessments
- [ ] Market size estimate from ≥1 credible source
- [ ] Clear answer on whether Pipe's surface overlaps or is differentiated
