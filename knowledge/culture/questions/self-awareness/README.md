# Self-awareness questions

Questions that score the **self-awareness** competency (see `../../dimensions/self-awareness.md`).

A good self-awareness question forces the candidate to describe how their style affects other people or to name a pattern in their own past behavior. Avoid "what's your biggest weakness?" — too well-rehearsed to score reliably.
