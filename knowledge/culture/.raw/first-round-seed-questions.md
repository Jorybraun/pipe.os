# Culture Fit Interview Questions

Source: [GitCodeCareer/culture-fit-interview-questions](https://github.com/GitCodeCareer/culture-fit-interview-questions), adapted from First Round Review.

---

## 1. Pivotal Transitions

- What changes are you looking for in your next role?
- Envision yourself three years from now — what's different about your work and how you work?
- Walk me through your job transitions: why you left each role and why you chose the next one.

---

## 2. Motivations

- Who do you most admire among past colleagues, and what specifically made them stand out?
- Describe a time you took unexpected initiative — something no one asked you to do.
- What's something genuinely great about your current or most recent company?
- What's your career highlight from the last five years?

---

## 3. Role Fit

- What skills are you good at but no longer want to use?
- What's the difference between a great performer and an outstanding one in your field?
- Name the top three traits needed for this role and rank yourself honestly on each.
- Describe your ideal role: responsibilities, team composition, culture.
- Where do you expect to move the needle in the first year?

---

## 4. Working with Others

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager — how did you handle it?
- Describe your best and worst boss. What made the difference?
- What elements of past team cultures would you preserve? Which would you leave behind?

---

## 5. Learning from Mistakes

- What's something important you've changed your mind about recently?
- What's the most valuable lesson you learned from a peer that you apply every day?
- Tell me about a significant mistake you made and how you addressed it.
- Walk me through three failures and what you took from each.
- What was your lowest career point? What did it teach you about yourself?

---

## 6. Self-Awareness

- What do coworkers sometimes get wrong about you?
- What's your personal superpower and how do you deploy it?
- What would someone who doesn't like working with you say about you?

---

## 7. Feedback Response

- Tell me about difficult feedback you've received and how you processed it.
- *(Interviewer delivers live feedback during the interview and observes the response)*

---

## 8. Passion

- What have you been obsessively learning or exploring lately?
- What do you care deeply about outside of work?
- What was your first job — the one not on your resume? What did it teach you?

---

## 9. Curveballs

- Why shouldn't we hire you?
- If you could change one thing to make this team 10x better, what would it be?
- Teach me something in five minutes.

---

## 10. Thinking Style

- If you were CEO for a day, what three things would you invest in to accelerate growth?
- Design a product that helps people find apartments. Walk me through your thinking.
- Give me ten ways to make pizza delivery faster.

---

## 11. Personality Indicators (MBTI-adjacent)

- Do you feel energised or drained after a big social event?
- Do you prefer working from abstract theory or concrete facts?
- When something goes wrong, do you focus more on how it felt or what you learned?
- Do you prefer to reach a conclusion quickly or keep gathering information?

---

## 12. Flip the Script

- What should we tell you about what it's actually like to work here?
- If you were hiring for this role, what attributes would matter most?
- What question haven't I asked that you'd want to answer?
