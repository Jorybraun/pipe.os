# Collaboration Style

**Type:** culture-profile dimension
**Scale:** 1-5 slider
**Evidence required:** verbatim quotes

---

## What this slider captures

How the candidate prefers to work with other people. Distinct from the collaboration *competency* (which measures skill at working with people) — this captures the environmental preference.

---

## Anchors

- **1 — Solo / async.** Prefers solo deep work. Communication is mostly written and asynchronous. Pairing is rare and purposeful.
- **2 — Mostly solo.** Solo work as default with regular but bounded sync interactions.
- **3 — Balanced.** Comfortable in both solo and paired modes. Chooses based on task.
- **4 — Mostly sync.** Regular pairing, real-time communication as default, comfortable with high interrupt rates.
- **5 — Sync-first.** Pairing as the default working mode. High comfort with real-time interruption. Describes solo work as less productive or less enjoyable.

---

## Signal sources

- **Low:** language around "deep work," "writing it up," "documenting," "leaving a comment on the PR," async tools
- **High:** language around "hopping on a call," "pairing," "working together in the room," "walking over to X's desk"

This dimension is especially important to match because mismatch causes daily friction — a pair-first candidate on a write-it-up team (or vice versa) will feel isolated or interrupted constantly.
