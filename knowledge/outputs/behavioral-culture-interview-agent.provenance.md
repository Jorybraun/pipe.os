## Key Limitation Notes
1. QWK 0.62 benchmark is from MMI (healthcare admissions), not STAR-format behavioral job interviews. No public STAR-specific scoring benchmark exists as of April 2026.
2. Mistral-specific performance on behavioral rubric scoring was not found in the literature; the AI/NLP findings apply to frontier models generally.
3. Illinois AIVIA text-only applicability requires employment counsel confirmation; the 2025 expansion (HB 3773) is new and untested in case law.
4. P-O fit → performance correlation (ρ = .15) is a critical caveat for any culture-fit scoring system; attitudinal predictions are much stronger.
