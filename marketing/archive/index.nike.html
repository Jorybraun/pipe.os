01 · The System

Three modules. One surface.

PIPE replaces the seven-tab hiring stack with a single graph-backed workbench. Nothing leaves the window. Nothing is copy-pasted. State is real.

◆ MODULE 01 · Ontology

Sourcing is a graph problem.

Ingest every résumé in your pipeline into a shared ontology — skills, teams, commits, domains as structural nodes, not keyword strings. Filter candidates by the shape of their career, not their vocabulary. Deterministic, explainable, reproducible. No LLM review.

◆ MODULE 02 · Review

Interviews on real work.

Every challenge is a live open-source pull request. Candidates read real code, debate real decisions with the PR author, and ship fixes that land in the upstream repo. The community gets the PR. You get the signal.

Fair — but useSyncExternalStore re-subscribes on subscribe identity, not getSnapshot. getSnapshot is called every render anyway; that's intentional. You might be thinking of useEffect?
