Every candidate gets a real pull request and a real opposing voice. Below is a scored fragment — the same surface the scorer reads. No games, no whiteboards, no fizzbuzz.

PR #2847 — replace useEffect sync with useSyncExternalStore 3 files · +48 −62 Code Review
Reviewer Candidate · turn 1
useTabVisibility.ts · L24 The useSyncExternalStore subscribe signature is correct — but you're not memoizing getSnapshot. Every render re-subscribes. Stabilize it with useCallback, or lift it out of the component.
Author PIPE agent · senior persona
Fair, but useSyncExternalStore re-subscribes on subscribe identity, not getSnapshot. getSnapshot is called every render anyway — that's intentional. You might be thinking of useEffect?
Reviewer Candidate · turn 2
You're right about subscribe vs. snapshot — I'll drop that. But the snapshot still reads document.visibilityState, which mutates between renders under concurrent mode and breaks tearing guarantees. That's the fix I want, even if my first reason was wrong.

02 · Receipts

Research-grounded. Not vibes-driven.

Every decision in PIPE traces back to a finding in our research brief. Three that matter most — and the citations behind them.

01

Human-expert reliability.

BARS-anchored rubrics, cross-family LLM-judge scoring, offline Sonnet 4.6 oracle. Panel agreement against human experts Cohen's κ ≥ 0.76 — the high end of inter-rater reliability, not a demo number.

Hodges 1988 · Landis & Koch 1977
02

Agentic consistency.

Every author-agent turn is classified by a separate-family model — Gemma guards Qwen, Qwen guards Gemma — across four axes: bug-disclosure, tone, knowledge boundary, pushback deviation. Drift is caught before the candidate sees it.

Panickssery 2024 · NeurIPS · cross-family principle
03

Built for the law.

Evidence-linked scoring. Disclosed agent use. Non-AI alternative path. NYC Local Law 144 bias-audit ready. EU AI Act Annex III human-oversight baked in from day one, not bolted on at deal-close. Compliance is a selling advantage, not a tax.
